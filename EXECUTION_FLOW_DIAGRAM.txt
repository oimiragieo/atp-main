EXECUTION STATISTICS
================================================================================

Total time in /v1/ask endpoint:
  • Pre-routing: ~10ms
  • Model selection: ~5ms
  • Response generation: simulated based on latency_slo_ms
    (with `await asyncio.sleep()` to match SLO)
  • Metrics calculation: ~5ms
  • Total: mostly simulated latency


Response size:
  • Plan JSON: ~200 bytes
  • Chunks: 120-180 "lorem" strings
  • Final response: ~1KB


Metrics in response:
  • tokens_in: always 30 (hardcoded)
  • tokens_out: 120 or 180 (hardcoded)
  • latency_ms: simulated (asyncio.sleep)
  • cost_usd: calculated (tokens / 1000 * cost_per_1k)
  • quality_score: random (0.7-0.9)
